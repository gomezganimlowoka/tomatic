About Us

AI-powered plant health

Meet Tomatoic

An intelligent diagnostic tool that helps farmers and gardeners detect tomato leaf diseases early — before they spread and damage your harvest. Upload a photo, get instant results.

What we offer

Instant diagnosis
Upload a leaf image and receive a disease prediction within seconds using a trained deep learning model.
Cause & context
Understand what triggered the disease — fungal, bacterial, viral, or environmental — with clear explanations.
Prevention tips
Get actionable guidance on treatments and prevention methods to protect your crop and stop further spread.

How it works

1
Capture a leaf image
Take a clear, close-up photo of the affected tomato leaf using your phone or camera.
2
Upload to Tomatoic
Submit the image through the app — no account required, just drag, drop, and go.
3
AI analyzes the leaf
Our deep learning model scans the image for visual patterns associated with known tomato diseases.
4
Get your diagnosis
Receive the disease name, severity indication, likely causes, and recommended treatment steps immediately.

Our mission is to put the power of AI in the hands of every farmer — from smallholder growers to large-scale producers. By enabling early detection of tomato diseases, Tomatoic helps reduce crop loss, lower pesticide overuse, and protect livelihoods that depend on a healthy harvest.
